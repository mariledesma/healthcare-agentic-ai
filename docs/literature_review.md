# Literature Review

This literature review focuses on recent work in clinical LLM agents,
multi-agent healthcare systems, tool-using agents, simulated clinical
evaluation, EHR interaction, and safety-aware clinical decision support.

The papers were selected to support the design and evaluation of the
Supervised Agentic AI Discharge Coordinator, particularly its use of
specialized agents, structured tool use, modular reasoning, and offline
case-based evaluation.

| Paper | Year | Why it fits |
|---|---:|---|
| **AgentClinic: a multimodal benchmark for tool-using clinical AI agents** | 2026 | Very relevant to simulated clinical agent evaluation and tool use. ([Nature][1]) |
| **PhysicianBench: Evaluating LLM Agents in Real-World EHR Environments** | 2026 | Directly relevant to agents working inside EHR-style environments. ([arXiv][2]) |
| **HealthFlow: automating electronic health record analysis via a strategically self-evolving multi-agent framework** | 2026 | Relevant to multi-agent EHR workflows, governed planning, and complex health-data pipelines. ([Nature][3]) |
| **MAP: evaluation and multi-agent enhancement of large language models for inpatient pathways** | 2026 | Especially relevant because your project is also an inpatient workflow system with specialized agents. ([Nature][4]) |
| **AgentRx: A Benchmark Study of LLM Agents for Multimodal Clinical Prediction Tasks** | 2026 | Useful for comparing single-agent versus multi-agent clinical architectures. ([arXiv][5]) |
| **LLM-Based Multi-Agent Systems for Clinical Workflows: A Survey of AI Hospitals** | 2026 | Very close to your topic: roles, handoffs, EHR-grounded tools, safety gates, and audit-ready workflows. ([ACL Anthology][6]) |
| **HealthAgentBench: A Unified Benchmark Suite of Realistic Agentic Healthcare Environments** | 2026 | Useful for evaluation design and realistic multi-step healthcare tasks. ([arXiv][7]) |
| **Benchmarking large language model-based agent systems for clinical decision tasks** | 2026 | Direct benchmark of clinical agent systems and useful for your evaluation section. ([Nature][8]) |
| **MedAgentBench: A Realistic Virtual EHR Environment to Benchmark Medical LLM Agents** | 2025 | Extremely relevant to your EHR-oriented agent prototype and benchmark design. ([arXiv][9]) |
| **AI Hospital: Benchmarking Large Language Models in a Multi-agent Medical Interaction Simulator** | 2025 | Useful for simulated multi-agent clinical workflows and evaluation. ([ACL Anthology][10]) |
